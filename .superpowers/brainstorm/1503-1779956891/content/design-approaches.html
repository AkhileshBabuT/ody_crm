Pros

  • Dark sidebar anchors navigation, light content is easy to read

Cons

My recommendation: Option C (Hybrid Mode). It gives you the polished, professional feel of a dark sidebar for focused navigation while keeping the content area light and scannable — ideal for a CRM that all roles use throughout the day. The "Live" badge and notification system also set up nicely for the real-time features you want.
